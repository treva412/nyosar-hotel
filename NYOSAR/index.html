ABOUT NYOSAR

Comfort is the whole point.

Nyosar sits on a quiet, gated compound with its own parking, gardens and a view over the hills. It was built as a place people actually return to for a wedding, a Sunday swim with the kids, a resturant where they order there favorite dishes.

Every part of the compound is designed to be used together: check into a room, swim before lunch, steam off in the sauna, then sit down to a meal without ever leaving the gate.

Beyond the facilities, Nyosar Hotel offers a quiet, peaceful, and well-aerated environment that allows guests to escape the noise and pressure of everyday life. The calm atmosphere creates the perfect setting for rest, relaxation and a refreshing stay.
