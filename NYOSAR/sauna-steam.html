SAUNA & STEAM

Built for exactly one thing: switching off

A timber sauna and steam room, tucked away from the pool and the restaurant noise.

THE SAUNA

Twenty minutes to reset

The sauna is lined floor to ceiling in timber, with tiered benches so you can sit lower for a gentler heat or higher for the full effect. It's a small, quiet room — no music, no queue, just heat and a bench.

Pair it with the steam room next door, or use it on its own after a swim or a gym session to ease into the rest of the evening.
